Delete Your Account

We respect your right to control your data. Follow the steps below to request permanent deletion of your AudLabs account and associated data.

How to Request Deletion

To request deletion of your AudLabs account, send an email to hello@audlabs.io with the subject line "Account Deletion Request", using the same email address you registered with on AudLabs.

Include the following in your request:

  • The email address associated with your AudLabs account
  • A brief confirmation that you want your account permanently deleted

We will process your request and confirm deletion within 7 business days.

What May Be Retained

For legal, tax, and fraud-prevention purposes, we may retain transaction and payment records (such as completed purchases) for a limited period as required by applicable financial regulations, even after account deletion. This data is retained securely and is not used for any other purpose.
